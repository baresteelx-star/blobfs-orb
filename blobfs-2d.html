      Drag an orb onto another and release to merge. Same kind merges on drop. Different kinds need a shared tag and your confirmation (amber glow, Accept / Keep apart). Without a shared tag they bounce apart. Pull apart before release to cancel. Physics may propose, never commit. The lime orb is a zorb (a zip): open it to see its contents, unpack copies them into a new folder orb. Save Orb writes a real .orb zip (demo.orb.zip: MAGIC, records.jsonl, each payload stored once under objects/ named by its SHA-256), which Open .orb reloads. Export history downloads the event log as readable text (blobfs-history.txt). The .orb also keeps the undo history and pending suggestions. Files you add, import or drop onto the stage are stored as their real bytes (sample-disk files have none and are marked as placeholders). Files of 4 GiB or more are refused (zip32 limit). Opens, plays and exports are logged as local events; nothing is sent anywhere.
